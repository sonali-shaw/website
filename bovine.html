Bovine Body Condition Scoring
A deep learning system that predicts bovine body condition scores (BCS) from aerial images, built over a summer with Argonne National Laboratory and the UChicago Data Science Institute. BCS is normally assessed by an expert eyeballing and palpating an animal — subjective, inconsistent between observers, and slow to do at herd scale. This project reframes it as a computer vision problem: a fine-tuned ResNet18 trained on aerial imagery to give farmers a fast, repeatable, hands-off way to monitor livestock health and catch problems earlier.
Highlights
  • Fine-tuned ResNet18 evaluated across multiple image-preprocessing filters (median, Laplacian, gradient-angle) to isolate which visual cues actually drive accurate scoring
  • Compared classification against regression framings of the same problem, informing which one a real deployment should use
  • Explored AI-assisted labeling strategies to work around the lack of ground-truth BCS data — a common bottleneck in applied agricultural ML
